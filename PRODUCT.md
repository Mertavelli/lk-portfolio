# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are owners, managing partners, and senior decision-makers at strategy and management consulting boutiques, plus comparable professional-services firms. They arrive to assess whether Louis understands how their work actually happens and can help them improve it with AI without removing necessary human judgment.

## Product Purpose

louiskarakas.com is Louis Karakas’ personal portfolio and point of contact. It shows selected work, personality, and a focused AI Strategy & Engineering practice: understanding operating workflows, deciding where AI creates real leverage, and building the systems that make the change real.

## Positioning

AI Strategy & Engineering. Louis helps businesses rethink how work is done with AI, then designs and implements the software, interfaces, and review systems required. The work connects strategic diagnosis with technical delivery; it is not a catalogue of disconnected AI tools.

## Operating Context

The relevant work includes research, analysis, knowledge search, document creation, repetitive information gathering, review, and decision-making. The approach is Assess → Diagnose → Prescribe → Implement: map the present workflow; distinguish genuine leverage from tasks that should remain human-led; define roles, data, system boundaries, and priorities; then ship working software.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a Next.js 14 / React 18 project using Contentful for existing portfolio content. The new expertise route uses code-owned editorial content and must not disrupt the project-led homepage or existing contact flow. No productivity guarantees, invented statistics, client claims, or fabricated evidence. A public research PDF may be linked from the route.

## Brand Commitments

The site remains personal, editorial, premium, restrained, technical, and direct. Its language avoids agency hype and vague transformation claims. It keeps the existing Studio Freight-derived visual system: black and off-white foundations, subtle grey rules, green accent, large Neue Montreal type, mono labels, measured motion, and responsive portfolio behavior.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/POSITIONING.md` is the current positioning source.
- `docs/Collaborative_AI_in_Private_Markets__A_Human_in_the_Loop_Editor_for_Investment_Workflows-2.pdf` is Louis’ research paper on schema-driven, human-in-the-loop document workflows.
- The research supports claims about structured generation, validation, retrieval, review, and the limits of fully autonomous output. It does not support commercial performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Start with how work happens today.
- AI is useful when it changes a workflow, not when it adds another tab.
- Not every task should be automated.
- High-stakes outputs must be inspectable, editable, and verifiable.
- Build the system only after the problem is understood.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic document structure, visible keyboard focus, adequate contrast, descriptive link labels, and motion that respects reduced-motion preferences. Content must remain understandable without animated state.
