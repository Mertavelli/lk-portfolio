# Positioning

## Core Positioning

**AI Strategy & Engineering**

I work at the intersection of strategy and engineering.

I help businesses understand where AI can create real leverage, redesign the relevant workflows, and build the systems required to make that new operating model real.

The goal is not to add more isolated AI tools. The goal is to make AI part of how the business actually operates.

## Primary Audience

The current commercial focus is on:

- Strategy and management consulting boutiques
- Other knowledge-intensive professional-services businesses
- Owner-led and partner-led firms
- Typically small to mid-sized teams where decision-makers are directly accessible

The ideal buyer is usually:

- Founder
- Managing Partner
- Partner
- Managing Director
- Owner / Geschäftsführer

## Problem Hypothesis

In many consulting businesses, significant advisor time is still spent on:

- Research
- Analysis
- Slide creation
- Internal knowledge search
- Repetitive information gathering
- Preparing structured outputs from fragmented inputs

These activities are valuable, but parts of the workflow can often be redesigned with AI so that professionals spend more time on judgment, client work, synthesis, and decision-making.

Do not assume every workflow should be automated.

The starting point is always to understand how the work happens today.

## Approach

**Assess → Diagnose → Prescribe → Implement**

### Assess

Understand how the business and team work today.

Look at:

- where time is spent
- where information moves
- where people repeatedly search, copy, structure, compare, or summarize
- where decisions require human judgment
- where current tools create friction

### Diagnose

Determine where AI can create meaningful leverage and where it cannot.

Do not automate for the sake of automation.

### Prescribe

Define:

- the target workflow
- human vs. AI responsibilities
- required data and integrations
- system boundaries
- implementation priorities

### Implement

Build the actual system when implementation makes sense.

This can include:

- agentic workflows
- retrieval systems
- structured interfaces
- integrations
- human-in-the-loop review
- custom AI software
- production deployment

## Strategic Principles

- Start with the business problem, not the model or tool.
- AI should change the workflow, not simply add another tab.
- Human judgment stays where it matters.
- High-stakes outputs should be inspectable, editable, and verifiable.
- Prefer structured systems over uncontrolled free-form generation.
- Do not maximize autonomy by default.
- Build only after the problem is understood.
- Strategy and implementation should connect.
